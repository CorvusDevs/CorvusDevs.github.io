Case study · Uruguay

AleteaFrom finding help to following through.

Two connected projects for Aletea: a public website that helps people find where to start, and an internal tool that helps the team carry requests forward.

Role
Experience design and development
Collaboration
With the Aletea team

01 / Public website

Give people a clear starting point.

We organized the site around what visitors come to do: find guidance, check the agenda, or explore resources. People can start with a question without needing to know how the association is organized.

Visit the public website↗

Three routes into the site

  • GuidanceFind the right way to get in touch.
  • AgendaSee activities and dates in one place.
  • ResourcesFind guides and practical materials.

02 / Internal coordination

Keep the next step visible.

The internal tool gives the team a place to receive requests, assign responsibility and record what happens next.

Design and development by CorvusDevs, in collaboration with the Aletea team. The team brought its day-to-day work and feedback to the design.
