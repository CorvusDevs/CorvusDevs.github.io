01 Public website

A simpler path to guidance.

For a first-time visitor, “I need guidance” is clearer than the name of a department. That became the starting point for the website.

The decision
Organize navigation around what people come to do: ask for guidance, find an activity or look up a resource.
What people can do today
Find where to ask for guidance, check dates or browse resources from one website.

02 Internal coordination

Keep each request moving.

The work does not end when a form is submitted. The team needs to see what came in, who is handling it and what happens next.

The decision
Bring intake, routing, ownership and follow-up together in a workflow the team can pick back up.
What people can do today
A request can move from a form to the team responsible for it, with a record of actions and next steps.
