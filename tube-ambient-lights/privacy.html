Tube Ambient Lights for Safari privacy policy

Updated November 11, 2026. This policy describes the current Safari adaptation, not the upstream browser extension.

Video processing and permissions

Tube Ambient Lights for Safari processes the playing video's colors locally on your device to draw ambient lighting. Automatic startup is selected by default, but runs only after you enable the extension and grant access to the supported YouTube sites, https://www.youtube.com/* and https://m.youtube.com/*. No permission request opens automatically and no website access is granted automatically. A previous choice to turn automatic startup off is preserved. Manual startup applies to the supported YouTube tab you select. Automatic startup uses only the host permissions you have granted. You can turn automatic startup off in the extension settings, stop the current light from the popup, and manage or revoke website access in Safari's extension settings.

The extension reads the selected page's URL and video ID to keep actions and settings attached to the intended tab and video. It reads player state and video dimensions to render lighting and provide local diagnostics. It does not send those details, video frames or colors to the developer or to an analytics or crash-reporting service.

YouTube still serves its own pages and media, and links you choose to open are handled by their destination services under their own privacy policies.

Locally stored settings

The extension stores settings, presets, automatic-start preferences and unfinished settings drafts using the browser's local extension storage. The companion app stores its own onboarding and interface preferences on your device. The app uses UserDefaults for its own preferences, not to read another app's information. These settings are not synchronized through a developer server.

Settings remain locally until changed or the corresponding stored app/browser data is removed. Turning off website access stops permitted page access; it does not by itself delete preferences or files you exported.

Diagnostics and files you choose to export

The app does not automatically send crash reports. There is no Sentry reporting, developer analytics or tracking service in this adaptation.

If you open a local diagnostic report, it can include the current page URL and video ID, app build, video dimensions and playback position/state, renderer and performance information, settings and recent error messages. Recent errors are kept in a bounded in-memory list; URL text is removed from those error messages, but the diagnostic report has separate URL and video ID fields.

You can inspect the report before saving or sharing it. Diagnostic and settings exports are files you choose to save locally. The app does not upload them or send them to support. If you choose to share a file using another app, review its contents and remove anything you do not want the recipient to see. Saved files remain until you delete them using Finder, Files or your chosen file manager, including any copies you created.

Data collection and contact

The developer does not receive browsing history, video details, settings or diagnostic files through the app. There is no developer account, advertising identifier or cross-app tracking feature. Because this app does not send that data to the developer, there is no developer-held copy of it for the app to delete.

For privacy or support questions, you may email corvusdevs@outlook.com using your own email app. Any information you choose to include will be shared with that recipient through your email provider. Include only what is needed for your question; do not send passwords or account tokens.
