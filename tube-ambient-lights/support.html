Light stopped after an interruption

Play the video and return to its tab. Check that the extension is still enabled and the site is allowed. If the player reports a graphics warning, reload that video page once and start again. Repeated graphics failures stop automatic recovery to avoid a loop. You do not need to reset your settings or quit Safari.

Picture in picture and layouts

On Mac, General settings includes Picture in picture and Enable in layouts. These control the page's glow, not light around Safari's floating window. On iPhone and iPad, lighting surrounds inline video and pauses in the system fullscreen player and Picture in Picture. Return the video to the page to resume lighting.
