Tube Ambient Lights for Safari

The App Store release is being prepared. No download is available yet.

macOS

Add a soft glow around your YouTube videos in Safari. Tube Ambient Lights for Safari follows the colors of the playing video and processes the lighting locally on your Mac.

iOS / iPadOS

Add a soft, color-matched glow around supported YouTube videos playing within the Safari webpage on your iPhone or iPad. Tube Ambient Lights for Safari processes the lighting locally on your device.

Processed on your device. No analytics.
