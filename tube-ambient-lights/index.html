02 / Features

Your video sets the mood.

Color that follows the scene

The glow adapts to the colors of supported videos as you watch.

Make it your kind of glow

Adjust spread, softness, brightness, and saturation for a subtle accent or a brighter glow.

Processed on your device

The extension doesn’t send video frames or browsing data to CorvusDevs, and it doesn’t require an account.

03 / Getting started

Ready in three steps.

  1. 01

    Download the app

    Find it on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

  2. 02

    Enable it in Safari

    Turn on Tube Ambient Lights in Safari Extensions and allow access to YouTube.

  3. 03

    Open a video

    Play a supported YouTube video in Safari. The effect starts automatically by default.

Before you download

Does it work in the YouTube app or with Shorts?

No. It’s designed for supported videos playing within the YouTube webpage in Safari, not Shorts or the native YouTube app.

What about fullscreen or Picture in Picture?

On iPhone and iPad, the glow pauses in the system fullscreen player and in Picture in Picture.

What if a video doesn’t show the effect?

Check that Safari has granted access to YouTube. Some protected videos may not support the effect.
